# KPIs (v0.1)

## Operational KPIs
1) **Avg Shipping Time**
- Definition: Average of `Shipping times`
- Why it matters: delivery speed performance

2) **Avg Lead Time**
- Definition: Average of `Lead times` (and/or `Lead time` after cleaning)
- Why it matters: supply responsiveness

3) **Stock Availability Rate (%)**
- Definition: % of records where `Availability` indicates available
- Why it matters: inventory health & customer satisfaction

4) **Order Fulfillment Volume**
- Definition: Sum of `Order quantities`
- Why it matters: operational throughput

## Cost KPIs
5) **Total Shipping Cost**
- Definition: Sum of `Shipping costs`
- Why it matters: logistics cost control

6) **Avg Shipping Cost per Order**
- Definition: AVG(`Shipping costs`)
- Why it matters: unit cost efficiency

7) **Total Manufacturing Cost**
- Definition: Sum of `Manufacturing costs`
- Why it matters: production efficiency & cost management

8) **Total Transportation/Route Cost**
- Definition: Sum of `Costs`
- Why it matters: route planning and transport optimization

## Quality KPIs
9) **Avg Defect Rate (%)**
- Definition: Average of `Defect rates`
- Why it matters: manufacturing quality performance

10) **Inspection Pass Rate (%)**
- Definition: % of records where `Inspection results` = Pass (or equivalent)
- Why it matters: quality control effectiveness
